Questions

Before you book.

Do I need to be technical?

No. You need to know your own work: what you do each week and how you like it done. I handle the setup, and the handover includes a plain-English guide and a recorded walkthrough. Adding a new command later takes a short written description, not code.

Will it really sound like me?

It writes from samples of your own writing and a voice profile we build together, then checks every draft against that profile and against common AI-writing habits. You still review everything before it goes out. Generic output usually comes from a blank chat with nothing of yours to work from.

Is my client data safe?

The system runs inside your own AI subscription and your own files. Privacy rules are part of the setup, including what it must never store or share. I don't keep copies of your client material, and I'm happy to sign an NDA before we start.

Which AI does it run on?

Claude, made by Anthropic. It's the platform I run my own system on every day, so it's the one I can stand behind. If you use ChatGPT today, you can keep it for other work; Autonomous Mode needs a Claude subscription, from $20 a month.

Isn't this just better prompts or a custom GPT?

Prompts and custom GPTs help with one task at a time, and most stop being used within weeks. Autonomous Mode is the layer around them: what the AI knows about your practice, the jobs it runs on command and the rules it follows, kept current as your work changes.

Why not wait until AI is built into my tools?

Built-in AI will know your documents. It won't know your clients, your offers or how you write unless something tells it. The knowledge layer you build now carries over to whatever tools come next, so the work isn't wasted when they arrive.

I don't have time to set this up.

Your time goes into one intake call, a short review at handover and using it on work you were doing anyway. I do the building, and your first command runs within 48 hours. Afterwards it takes about 15 minutes a week, mostly the weekly review it runs with you.

What if you don't deliver?

Then you get your money back. Your first command is due by day 2. If it isn't running on your own inbox and calendar by day 7, I refund everything you've paid. All I need from you is the intake call and access on day 1.

What if leads still slip?

Then I keep working. Once it's running, no open lead or proposal should go more than 2 working days without a follow-up drafted for you. If one does, I fix it at no charge until it doesn't. Your part: send leads and proposals through the inbox it reads or add them to your pipeline, decide on each draft and do the 15-minute weekly review.

Will it save me time as well?

Yes. The morning brief, the memory and the guardrails take a lot of admin off your plate, and on the review call a month in we count your hours too. Its main job is the pipeline, though: the follow-ups and asks that bring in your next piece of work.

Privacy

Last updated 30 October 2026

This page covers two things I run: this website, and gws-cli, a private tool I use with my own Google accounts. I'm Ato Sam, and you can reach me at ato@theatosam.com.

This website

The site sets no cookies. It uses Vercel Web Analytics to count page visits (page, referring site, country, device type) without cookies and without storing your IP address. Vercel, which hosts it, also keeps standard server logs (such as IP address and browser type) to run and protect the site.

If you apply for an install or ask for the starter kit, your email address and your answers go to Kit (kit.com), the service I use to send email. I use them to send you the kit, to review your application and to send occasional emails about my work. Every email has an unsubscribe link, and you can ask me to delete your details at any time.

If you book a call, Calendly handles the booking under its own privacy policy. If you email me, I use your message only to reply to you.

gws-cli

gws-cli is a command-line tool that connects to Google's APIs. I use it on my own computer, with my own Google accounts. It isn't offered to anyone else, and no one else signs in to it.

  • What it accesses. When I ask it to, it reads and changes data in my own Gmail, Google Drive, Calendar, Docs, Sheets, Slides and Tasks.
  • Where the data goes. It runs on my computer. I don't run a server for it, and it doesn't send Google data to one. When I use it with my AI assistant (Claude Code, made by Anthropic), the data I pull in is processed by that assistant to complete my request.
  • What I never do. Sell the data, share it with advertisers or use it for advertising.
  • Sign-in. Sign-in tokens are stored on my computer. Access can be removed at any time from Google Account permissions.

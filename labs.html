Questions before you say yes

What is the preview?

We start with a short call so I know what you do. Then I build a first version of your site before you pay anything. You see your name, your words and your pictures on a real page. Like it, and you pay half. Don’t, and you owe nothing.

Who owns the site?

You do. The address is registered in your name, and you get a copy of every file. If you ever want the site moved to your own account, I do that free when you ask.

Will a website bring me customers?

I can’t promise customers or revenue. A good site helps people who are already searching for you to find you, trust you and reach you in one tap. That is what I build, and the preview lets you judge it before you pay.

What does the Google profile set-up cover?

Your business profile claimed and verified, with the phone number, opening hours and details put right. It is the listing people see on Google Maps and in search results, and where they tap to call you or get directions.

I already have a website. Can you still help?

Often, yes. If your site works but the businesses above you on Google have more reviews, the gap is reviews, not the site. The Review Engine asks every customer for a review after each finished job. Ask about it and I’ll send you a free comparison against the top three in your own search.
